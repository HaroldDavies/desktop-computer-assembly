Component 1 of 6

Keyboard

All parts laid out. Scroll to start assembling.

  1. Place the structural plate
  2. Seat the PCB and controller
  3. Lay the membrane layer
  4. Fit the outer casing
  5. Press on the keycaps

What it does

The keyboard is the main input device. Pressing a key pushes a dome switch against the PCB matrix, and the controller chip sends that key's code to the computer.

Safety. Disassemble in reverse: unplug the cable, pull keycaps, unclip the casing, then lift each layer.

Component 2 of 6

Speaker System

All parts laid out. Scroll to start assembling.

  1. Mount the amplifier board
  2. Seat the magnetic motor
  3. Fit the woofer cone
  4. Fit the tweeter
  5. Snap on the front grille

What it does

A speaker turns an electrical signal into sound. The amplifier board boosts the signal, and the magnetic motor drives the woofer (low tones) while the tweeter plays high tones.

Safety. Disassemble in reverse: unplug power and audio, lift off the grille, unscrew the drivers, free the board.

Component 3 of 6

System Unit

All parts laid out. Scroll to start assembling.

  1. Mount the PSU and I/O shield
  2. Install the board, CPU and RAM
  3. Add the GPU and network card
  4. Fit the optical drive, HDD and SSD
  5. Route the cables
  6. Close the top cover and side panel

What it does

The system unit houses the computer's core parts. The motherboard links everything: the CPU processes data, RAM holds working memory, the GPU draws graphics and the PSU powers it all.

Safety. Disassemble in reverse. Power off, unplug, and wear an anti-static wrist strap first.

Component 4 of 6

Monitor

All parts laid out. Scroll to start assembling.

  1. Start with the outer bezel frame
  2. Stack the polarizer, color filter and liquid crystal
  3. Add the LCD/LED panel
  4. Fit the diffuser and LED backlight
  5. Connect the control and power boards
  6. Attach the stand and bracket

What it does

A flat-panel monitor shows the picture by stacking thin layers. LEDs at the back make light, the liquid crystal layer and color filter shape it into pixels, and the control board drives the panel.

Safety. Unplug the monitor first. Never press on the panel, and lay it face down on a soft cloth to work.

Component 5 of 6

Mouse

All parts laid out. Scroll to start assembling.

  1. Fit the base with Teflon feet
  2. Seat the PCB and sensor
  3. Solder in the microswitches
  4. Install the scroll wheel and encoder
  5. Clip on the top shell

What it does

The mouse is a pointing device. The optical sensor reads movement across the desk, the microswitches register clicks, and the scroll wheel's encoder counts each turn for the microcontroller.

Safety. Disassemble in reverse: remove the screws under the feet, lift the shell, then free the wheel and PCB.

Component 6 of 6

Whole Computer

All parts laid out. Scroll to start assembling.

  1. Stand the system unit on the desk
  2. Set the monitor in the middle
  3. Place the keyboard in front of it
  4. Put the mouse beside the keyboard
  5. Position the two speakers

What it does

The five finished components are set up together on a desk. The system unit does the computing, the monitor and speakers give output, and the keyboard and mouse send input back.

Safety. Power off and unplug everything before you connect cables. Plug the power cable in last, and reverse the order to take it apart.

Wrap up

Conclusion and safety tips

Every component is built from layers and modules that fit together in a set order, and come apart in the reverse order.

Power off and unplug

Shut down, then disconnect the power cable and all peripherals before opening the case.

Prevent static damage

Wear an anti-static wrist strap, or touch bare metal on the case, to discharge yourself.

Handle parts by the edges

Hold boards and cards by their edges. Never touch gold contacts or chips.

Reverse order, sort screws

Take parts out in the reverse of assembly order and keep screws in labelled containers.
